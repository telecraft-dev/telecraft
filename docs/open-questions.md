| OQ-21 | Revoking an issued licence, and what a compromised signing key costs. | OQ-23 | At-rest custody of per-tenant secret material in the hosted service: where the ciphertext lives, what encrypts it, who holds that key, and what a tenant administrator may read back (never the value). | OQ-24 | An Organisation's life after it is created: suspend, resume, retire. What suspension does to the Instance, to the OpAMP endpoint, and to the estate repository, and what retirement leaves behind. | carried | Raised by ADR-0069. Hosted billing (#196) and the licence's failure behaviour (ADR-0070 §4) both land on this and neither owns it: ADR-0070 §4 decides what an expired licence does, which is to refuse widening and never to stop serving, and this is what a deprovisioned Organisation does to collectors that are still connected. Needed before the first Organisation is retired, not before the first is created. ADR-0072 §10 answers the hosted half and narrows this to the self-managed one: an unpaid subscription refuses Authoring and never delivery, retirement is deliberate and notified and is the only thing that stops an Instance, and ADR-0072 §9 decides what retirement leaves behind.
